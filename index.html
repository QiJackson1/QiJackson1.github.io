About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science and Technology at the College of AI, Tsinghua University. My research focuses on Embodied AI for Robotics, Exoskeleton & Humanoid Robotics, PMSM Design & Control. I received my master's degree from Tsinghua's Department of Mechanical Engineering, where I built full-stack R&D experience spanning mechanical design, electric motors, and algorithms for exoskeleton robots, specialized motors, and sensorless control.

I build robotic systems for the real world: algorithms that respect hardware constraints, and electromechanical designs that create better conditions for learning-based control.
